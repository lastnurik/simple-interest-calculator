# Simple Interest Calculator

A Bash calculator that computes simple interest from a principal amount, an annual interest rate, and a time period in years.

## Formula

Simple interest = principal × annual rate × time / 100.

## Usage

Run `bash simple-interest.sh` and enter the principal, annual rate (as a percentage), and time in years when prompted. Decimal values are supported.

For example, a principal of 1000 at 5% per year for 2 years earns 100.00 in simple interest.
